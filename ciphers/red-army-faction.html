Why This Matters

The Bundesarchiv documents Stasi contacts with the RAF and the sheltering of ten former members in East Germany between 1980 and 1983. Those records establish relationships and support; they do not, by themselves, establish the particular one-time-pad system previously attributed to this case.

Historical Context

Evidence limit: This exhibit has no identified intercepted-message series, recovered RAF pad or technical manual supporting claims of hundreds of unreadable transmissions or a particular KGB-supplied key kit. Those claims are therefore not presented as established history.

The KGB Field OTP Format

The widget illustrates digitwise addition modulo 10 without carry. This operation is not “Fibonacci addition,” and the widget is not a reconstruction of an authenticated RAF communication procedure. A real one-time pad would need independent random secret key digits, sufficient length and no reuse.

The Stasi Files Exposure
Archive exposure post-reunification
Complexity: Metadata attack — cipher itself not broken

Stasi records became important evidence after German reunification. Archival proof of an organisational relationship and recovery of a cryptographic system are distinct findings; missing key material alone cannot prove that a specific one-time pad was used.
