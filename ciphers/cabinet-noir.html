Cabinet Noir — Black Chambers of Europe

Before satellites, Western powers ran state intercept bureaux — opening every diplomatic letter, deciphering it, and resealing the wax without a trace.

Why This Matters

European governments developed postal interception and cryptanalytic offices whose practices offer comparisons with later communications intelligence. Staff could open, copy, analyse and reseal selected letters. Practices and institutions differed by country and period; this was not one continuous organisation ancestral to every modern agency.

The French Cabinet Noir

Antoine Rossignol and his son Bonaventure served the French monarchy in cryptographic and cryptanalytic roles. Kahn dates Antoine’s celebrated Réalmont decipherment to 1628. Earlier European state cryptologists already existed, so the Rossignols should not be called the first professional cryptanalysts in Europe. Royal nomenclators also changed over time.

The Austrian Geheime Kabinettskanzlei

Vienna’s Geheime Kabinettskanzlei exploited access to postal routes and diplomatic correspondence. Its work combined physical interception, copying and decipherment. Reading selected dispatches could reveal negotiating positions, but does not establish access to every communication of every other power. Kahn describes the office’s closure in 1848.

Britain's Secret Office

British postal interception and the Decyphering Branch became subjects of controversy in 1844, notably after the opening of Giuseppe Mazzini’s letters. Kahn distinguishes the end of diplomatic interception in June from dissolution of the Decyphering Branch in October. That episode should not be described as the permanent end of all British state mail interception.

Comparing Postal and Electronic Interception

Postal opening and modern electronic interception share questions about access, secrecy, technical capability and oversight. Their institutions and legal settings differ. A comparison with Room 40, Bletchley Park or later agencies is useful, but a direct organisational chain from every black chamber to both GCHQ and NSA is not established.
