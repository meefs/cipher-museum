The Mathematics Under the Cryptography

The ground your
keys stand on

Every modern cipher in the museum rests on mathematics far older than any protocol. This hall shows that bedrock — the number theory, the hardness assumptions, the structures — with a working interactive for each, and an honest account of where the math stops being something a cryptographer directly uses.

What belongs here. These are foundations, not primitives. Different schemes use different parts of this mathematics; some exhibits provide context rather than a security assumption. For the deployable tools, see Modern Cryptography and Hall XI. This hall is for understanding why they work.
