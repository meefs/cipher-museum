Key distribution/authentication and operational comparisons now match corrected body scope.
- Great Cipher: replaced continuous-two-century-secrecy rhetoric and unsupported hill-climbing certainty; corrected Antoine-Bonaventure to Bonaventure's son; distinguished historical erase-previous-sign from the model's erase-letter rule. Replaced the unverified quoted night/mask order with a paraphrase matching Kahn's actual dispatch discussion. No other historical quotations were rewritten without this direct check. Model greedy parsing is labelled as model behavior.
- ECDSA states the verifier's x-coordinate reduction modulo n and makes modular division explicit in nonce-recovery equations; no engine change. Its metadata/hero describe signing keys without claiming a single PS3 master key.
- Columnar credits now give full titles for Gaines and Holden. No ebook links introduced.
